# How to Play Lecture Bets

Bet play-money points on what happens in this lecture. No real money.

## Start
Pick a username. You get **1,000 pts**.

## Bet
Each market is a yes/no question, like *"Prof reaches Slide 15 by 10:30"*.
Tap **+50**, **+100** or **+250** on the side you believe. Each button shows what it pays if you win.
You can bet as often as you like, but only on **one side** per market.

## Odds
The Referee sets the starting chance (e.g. Yes 30% → **3.33x**, No 70% → **1.43x**).
**You keep the odds you bet at.** Every bet then moves the odds for the next player:
the more points on a side, the lower its odds.

**Example:** you bet 100 on Yes at 3.33x. Others pile onto Yes and it drops to 2.00x.
Yes wins → you still get **333 pts** (100 × 3.33). Winnings can be more than everyone staked.

## Create a market
Tap **+ Create Market**. You become its **Referee**:
- You **can't bet** on it.
- You **decide the outcome**.
- You set the **starting chance** for each side, which sets the starting odds.
- You earn a **5% bonus** of all points staked. It's extra and doesn't reduce winnings.
- Optional: tap **+1** on the card each time the event happens (e.g. every "good contribution"), so bettors can see the count so far.

Pick a timer (30s–10m) or **No limit** (you close betting yourself).
On timed markets, **betting closes after 80% of the time**: in a 5-minute market nobody can bet during the last minute.

## Refunds
Everyone is refunded if the Referee doesn't decide in time:
- **Timed markets:** within 1 hour after the timer ends.
- **No-limit markets:** within 3 hours of creating the market.

## Fine print
Points reset when the server restarts or goes idle. Play fair: Referees, call it honestly!
