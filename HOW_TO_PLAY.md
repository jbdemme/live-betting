# How to Play Lecture Bets

Bet play-money points on what happens in this lecture. No real money.

## Start
Pick a username. You get **1,000 pts**.

## Bet
Each market is a yes/no question, like *"Prof reaches Slide 15 by 10:30"*.
Tap **+50**, **+100** or **+250** on the side you believe. Bet as often as you like.

## Odds
There are no fixed odds. Everyone's bets go into one pot, and the winners split it.
The fewer points on your side, the bigger your share. Odds (e.g. **2.40x**) update live.

**Example:** 300 pts on Yes, 100 on No. "No" wins → each No point pays 4x.

## Create a market
Tap **+ Create Market**. You become its **Referee**:
- You **can't bet** on it.
- You **decide the outcome**.
- You earn a **5% bonus** of the pot. It's extra, so winners get the full pot.

Pick a timer (30s–10m) or **No limit** (you close betting yourself).

## Refunds
Everyone is refunded if the Referee doesn't decide in time:
- **Timed markets:** within 60 seconds after betting closes.
- **No-limit markets:** within 3 hours of creating the market.

## Fine print
Points reset when the server restarts or goes idle. Play fair: Referees, call it honestly!
